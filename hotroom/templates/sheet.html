How these numbers were calculated

es = 6.105 × exp(17.27 × T ÷ (237.7 + T))

e = RH ÷ 100 × es

humidex = T + 0.5555 × (e − 10)

gamma = ln(RH ÷ 100) + 17.27 × T ÷ (237.7 + T)

dew point = 237.7 × gamma ÷ (17.27 − gamma)

Comfortable is below 30. Some discomfort is 30 to 39.9. Great discomfort is 40 to 45. Dangerous is above 45.

Humidex and dew point are rounded half away from zero to one decimal place. The humidity effect is the humidex minus the air temperature, also to one decimal place.
